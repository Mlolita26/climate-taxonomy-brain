How it works. Words are matched against the taxonomy's labels and synonyms (plural- and hyphen-insensitive, longest phrase first, five homonym guards). Then the pathway rule runs as in the PRMS tagging pipeline: an adaptation-specific term counts on its own; an adaptation-conditional term counts only through a qualifying relation to an anchor (stored in the taxonomy, or, with the proxy on, present in the same sentence); each counting term fills one of four pathway elements and the number of distinct elements gives the verdict: 4 Full, 3 or 2 Partial, 1 Minimal, 0 None. The mitigation axis runs the same way in parallel. What this page does not do is the model step of the pipeline: judging from-text relations and checking that a matched word really means the concept.
