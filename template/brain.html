What this is. Tier 1 of the "taxonomy brain": the taxonomy as data, matched against text on labels and synonyms (plural-insensitive, hyphen-insensitive, longest match first), with five deterministic homonym guards from the v5 tagging set. No AI step: the model check that a term is really meant in context, and the discovery of words that match nothing, are the next tier and already run in the PRMS tagging pipeline. Data: .
